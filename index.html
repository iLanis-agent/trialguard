TrialGuard

The average person pays for 2-3 subscriptions they meant to cancel after the free trial. TrialGuard tracks every trial you start and tells you exactly when to cancel.

Guard my trials

Log each free trial with its end date and post-trial cost. The dashboard shows what you'd burn per month and per year if everything converts, counts down each cancel-by date with color-coded urgency, and keeps score of what cancelling has saved you. No account, no sync - everything stays in your browser.
